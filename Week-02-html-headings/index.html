My Favorite Video Games

This page explains three of my favorite video games and why they mean so much to me.

Portal 2

Portal 2 is a unique sequel. It takes the simple portal mechanics that the original had perfected and mostly leaves them alone. The core of the game is still, undoubtedly, using the gun to get from here to there, and the addition of the surface-modifying gels adds some dynamism to the game, but it mostly adds story and depth. Just about at the halfway mark, you start to discover the audio logs of the founder of Aperture Science, Cave Johnson. Johnson reveals through his increasingly sick voice that he subjected his employees to numerous atrocities during testing, and the manufacturing process itself is killing him. It twists the whole game around and gives a lot of narrative depth to something that was merely hinted at before.

Persona 5 Royal

Persona 5 Royal is, frankly, the quickest 100 hours you can spend with a game. The game's supercharged anime cutscenes and style ensure that it stays visually fresh at every turn. The funky J-Pop soundtrack is probably the best video game soundtrack of the 2010s, and the deep battle mechanics never get old, adding new techniques and types through the first 70 hours of the game or so.

Mother 3

Mother 3 is simply Nintendo's most interesting game of the 21st century. Taking all the mechanics from Earthbound, but super-charging them with a unique rhyhtm game mechanic, the game also benefits from being 12 years newer than Earthbound. It features nicer graphics and more forgiving mechanics than its predecessor. Its simplified and streamlined mechanics, forced by the GBA's 4-button interface, cuts down a lot on friction, and the deeply emotional story gives it a punch that virtually no other Nintendo games have ever had.
