Pokemon Pokopia

The best Pokemon game in 15 years

Pokopia is the most exciting game in the series since the days of the Nintendo DS. Taking a fair amount of DNA from Minecraft and especially Animal Crossing, Omega-Force has eschewed the typical RPG format for a game forcused on building hospitable homes and environments for your monsters. While we have come to love the Animal Crossing villagers with time, the immediate buy-in of building a home for Bublasaur at the beginning of the game is a stronger hook than starting your AC game with the least weird-looking pig villager.
